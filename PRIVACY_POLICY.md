# Privacy Policy for YouTube Bookmarks Extension

**Last Updated: February 14, 2026**

## Overview
YouTube Bookmarks is a Chrome extension that helps users bookmark and annotate specific moments in YouTube videos. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## Data Collection
**We DO NOT collect, transmit, or share any personal data.**

### What Data is Stored Locally
The extension stores the following information **locally on your device only**:
- Video IDs of bookmarked YouTube videos
- Timestamps of your bookmarks
- Notes you add to bookmarks
- Video titles (for display purposes)

### Where Data is Stored
All data is stored locally using Chrome's `storage.local` API on your device. **No data is ever sent to external servers or third parties.**

## Permissions Explained

### storage
- **Purpose**: Save your bookmarks locally on your device
- **Usage**: Stores bookmark data in your browser's local storage
- **Data Access**: Only you have access to this data

### tabs
- **Purpose**: Communicate with YouTube tabs to control video playback
- **Usage**: Send commands to seek to bookmarked times and add visual markers
- **Data Access**: Only accesses YouTube tabs when you use the extension

### sidePanel
- **Purpose**: Display the bookmark interface in Chrome's side panel
- **Usage**: Shows the extension UI alongside YouTube videos
- **Data Access**: No data collection

### Host Permission (https://www.youtube.com/*)
- **Purpose**: Interact with YouTube video pages
- **Usage**: Add visual markers to progress bar and control video playback
- **Data Access**: Only accesses YouTube pages you visit, no data is transmitted

## Third-Party Services
**This extension does NOT use any third-party services, analytics, or tracking.**

## Data Retention
- Bookmarks are stored indefinitely on your device until you delete them
- You can delete individual bookmarks or clear all bookmarks at any time
- Uninstalling the extension will remove all stored data

## Children's Privacy
This extension does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age.

## Changes to Privacy Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above.

## Contact
If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the Chrome Web Store support page.

## Your Rights
You have the right to:
- View all data stored by the extension (stored locally in Chrome storage)
- Delete all data by uninstalling the extension or clearing bookmarks
- Use the extension without providing any personal information

## Compliance
This extension complies with:
- Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies
- Google API Services User Data Policy
- GDPR (no personal data collected)
- CCPA (no personal data sold or shared)

---

**Summary**: This extension stores bookmarks locally on your device only. No data is collected, transmitted, or shared with anyone.
